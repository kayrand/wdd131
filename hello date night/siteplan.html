Hello Date Night

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Hello Date Night

I picked "Hello Date Night" because it feels warm, friendly, and inviting, kind of like getting a fun text from your best friend with the perfect date idea. The name brings that little spark of excitement you get when you're planning something fun with someone you love, but it still feels casual and easy. I wanted it to be the kind of name that works for anyone, whether you're in a new relationship, married for years, or just looking for something different to do this weekend.

Site Purpose

Hello Date Night is a date night planning website that helps couples come up with fun, creative ideas based on what they're in the mood for. It's basically a one stop spot for date inspiration, personalized suggestions, and a place to save your favorite ideas for later.

The site will have three main pages:

  • Home Page: A welcoming landing page that introduces visitors to the site and shows off a few featured date ideas to get them excited.
  • Date Ideas Page: A full collection of date ideas pulled from a JavaScript array. Users can filter by category (indoor, outdoor, budget, food, and more) and save their favorites using localStorage so they can come back to them later.
  • Plan a Date Page: An interactive form where users share what they're looking for (budget, vibe, time, location) and get back a personalized date idea created with JavaScript.

Scenarios

Here are some questions a typical visitor might ask:

  • "What can my husband and I do for a budget friendly date night this weekend that doesn't involve another dinner and a movie?"
  • "It's pouring rain outside, what are some cozy and creative date ideas we can do at home tonight?"
  • "Can I save my favorite date ideas to a list so I can plan ahead for special occasions throughout the year?"

Color Schema

The Hello Date Night color palette is modern, romantic, and feminine, with a Pinterest-inspired feel.

Off-White, #F7F2EC

Used for the page background, creates a soft, clean canvas.

Soft Coral, #E89B8F

Used for accent details, dividers, and decorative elements.

Deep Rose, #9C4B5C

Used for headings, buttons, footer, and important text.

Navy Plum, #2E1F32

Used for body text and dark accents.

Typography

Hello Date Night uses two carefully selected Google Fonts.

Playfair Display

Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3) throughout the site. This serif font adds an elegant, romantic feel.

Lato

Used for all body text, paragraphs, navigation, and form elements. This clean, modern sans-serif font ensures excellent readability.
